Hi, I'm Andrew

I’m a 22-year-old graduate student in Information Technology at Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges. I didn’t arrive here by accident. My curiosity with computers started long before I knew what “IT” even meant. I’ve always been the type to click around, break things, fix them, and wonder how everything works behind the screen.

Over the years, that curiosity turned into something more intentional. What began as casually playing with programs and spending too much time on the internet slowly became a path I chose to take seriously. These days, I’m learning by building, exploring web development, UI/UX, and cybersecurity through hands-on projects and trial and error.

When I’m not coding, I write, edit videos, and dive into volleyball-related work, whether that’s analysis or building a website to visualize rankings. I also blog and contribute to Wikipedia, where research, clarity, and sourcing matter just as much as the final output.
